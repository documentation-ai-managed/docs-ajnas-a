## Overview

Google Analytics lets you monitor visitor behavior and marketing results.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Account">
    ````markdown
    Go to `https://analytics.google.com`, sign in or register.
    Click "Start measuring" and fill your business/site info.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Tracking ID">
    ````markdown
    Inside your property, find Tracking Info > Tracking Code.
    Copy your unique tracking script (e.g., UA-XXXXXXX).
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Your Site">
    ````markdown
    Paste the script just before `</head>` in your website HTML.
    Save and deploy your site.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Done

View your website data in the Google Analytics dashboard.
